# 01 — Brand Identity

## Name

**Aura Dracin**

| Part | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Aura** | Vibe, energy, the feeling a great Dracin scene gives you |
| **Dracin** | Indonesian slang for Drama Cina — instantly recognizable to the audience |

**Pronunciation:** *OW-rah DRA-chin*  
**Written style:** Two words, title case. Domain suggestions: `auradracin.com`, `auradracin.id`

---

## Tagline Options

Pick one for launch; others can rotate in social/SEO:

1. **"Nonton Dracin, rasain auranya."** ← recommended primary
2. "Dracin favoritmu, satu swipe aja."
3. "Vibes Dracin, tanpa ribet."
4. "Drama China, auranya beda."

---

## Brand Personality

| Trait | Expression |
|-------|------------|
| **Casual** | Copy feels like chat, not corporate marketing |
| **Fandom-driven** | Celebrates shared obsession with Dracin |
| **Inclusive** | Welcoming to new viewers and hardcore fans |
| **Mobile-native** | Built for phone, portrait, on-the-go watching |
| **Community-first** | Likes, comments, "lagi rame di kotamu" |

**Avoid:** Formal Indonesian (e.g. "Silakan menonton"), English-heavy UI, stiff KBBI-style SEO.

---

## Target Audience

### Primary: Gen Z (18–27)

- Grew up with TikTok/Reels; expects vertical video and swipe gestures
- Uses hashtags and comments as social currency
- Discovers content via trends and friends, not TV guides
- Bahasa Indonesia mixing (gaul) is natural

### Primary: Millennials (28–38)

- Nostalgic for K-drama/C-drama waves; often the "introducer" to friends
- Watches on commute or before sleep
- Values curated quality over infinite scroll noise
- Still mobile-heavy but may browse on desktop occasionally

### Secondary: Dracin curious

- Heard about a viral scene; needs low-friction entry
- Homepage categories and "Lagi Populer" serve as onboarding

---

## Tone of Voice (Bahasa Indonesia)

### Do

- "Lagi populer banget nih di Jakarta"
- "Dracin romance yang bikin baper"
- "Komen scene favoritmu!"
- "Belum punya akun? Daftar dulu, gratis kok."

### Don't

- "Silakan registrasi untuk mengakses fitur interaktif."
- "Video streaming platform untuk drama Tiongkok."
- Overuse English where Indonesian slang works better

---

## Logo Direction

**Wordmark:**
- `Aura` — lighter weight, off-white (`#F5F5F7`)
- `Dracin` — bold, primary rose (`#E91E8C`)

**Icon (optional):**
- Play triangle inside a soft circular glow (aura ring)
- Framed in 9:16 rounded rectangle hinting at portrait format

**Favicon:** Stylized "A" with subtle glow, works at 32×32.
